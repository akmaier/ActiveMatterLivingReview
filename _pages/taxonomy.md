The taxonomy is built from the data extracted for every included study, so it has no fixed shape.
Choose which attributes form the levels and in what order. The root is the whole corpus, and the
tree re-forms as you change it. Click a node to open or close it and to list its studies; drag,
scroll or use the controls to move and zoom. Attributes that can carry several labels (phenomena,
theory, interaction) place a study under each of its labels, so the counts at one level can add up
to more than the parent.
